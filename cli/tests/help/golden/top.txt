Terminal tester for the e-voice gateway¶
¶
Usage: ecli <COMMAND>¶
¶
Commands:¶
  stt      Stream speech to the gateway and print transcripts as they arrive¶
  tts      Stream text to the TTS gateway and play the voice as it is generated¶
  devices  List audio inputs; `*` marks the system default¶
  help     Print this message or the help of the given subcommand(s)¶
¶
Options:¶
  -h, --help     Print help¶
  -V, --version  Print version¶
