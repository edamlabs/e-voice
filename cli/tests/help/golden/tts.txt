Stream text to the TTS gateway and play the voice as it is generated¶
¶
Usage: ecli tts [OPTIONS] [TEXT]¶
¶
Arguments:¶
  [TEXT]  ¶
¶
Options:¶
      --url <URL>      TTS gateway base URL [env: ECLI_TTS_URL=] [default: ws://127.0.0.1:5600]¶
      --lang <LANG>    ¶
      --voice <VOICE>  Learned voice id; the server default when omitted¶
      --out <OUT>      Also save the received audio to this WAV file¶
      --mute           Do not play (with --out: just record)¶
  -h, --help           Print help¶
