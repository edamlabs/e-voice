Read a text aloud; the daemon records it and the speech service learns the voice¶
¶
Usage: ecli voice add [OPTIONS] <ID>¶
¶
Arguments:¶
  <ID>  Voice id: 1-64 chars of a-z, 0-9, '-' or '_'¶
¶
Options:¶
      --text <TEXT>        Text to read; the daemon's reading script for the language when omitted¶
      --lang <LANG>        Language of the reading script and of the sample; the daemon's when omitted [possible values: es, en]¶
      --silence <SILENCE>  Silence after speech that ends the recording (e.g. 2.5s); the daemon's default when omitted¶
      --max <MAX>          Longest clip (e.g. 30s, at most 30 s); the daemon's default when omitted¶
      --replace            Replace a voice with the same id¶
      --url <URL>          Daemon base URL [env: ECLI_DAEMON_URL=] [default: http://127.0.0.1:5700]¶
  -h, --help               Print help¶
