Speak a text through the daemon and wait until it has played¶
¶
Usage: ecli say [OPTIONS] <TEXT>¶
¶
Arguments:¶
  <TEXT>  Text to speak¶
¶
Options:¶
      --voice <VOICE>  Voice; the speech service's default when omitted¶
      --lang <LANG>    Language (es, en); the speech service's default when omitted¶
      --url <URL>      Daemon base URL [env: ECLI_DAEMON_URL=] [default: http://127.0.0.1:5700]¶
  -h, --help           Print help¶
